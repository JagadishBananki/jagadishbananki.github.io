Philosophy

"Humans perceive the world by dividing it into independent parts. Our art dissolves those boundaries."

teamLab (est. 2001) is an interdisciplinary collective of artists, programmers, engineers, CG animators, mathematicians, and architects. Together they pursue art, science, technology, and the natural world.

Their immersive environments respond to visitor presence and movement — each experience unique. Works are non-static: they change, breathe, and evolve in real time, blurring the boundary between the artwork and the observer.

The collective believes boundaries between self and world are artificial — art can dissolve them, revealing a continuous, borderless, interconnected whole.

01

Borderlessness

Works flow into one another without walls or frames. Art exists in continuity — each piece breathing into the next, creating an uninterrupted immersive space.

02

Ultra-Subjective Space

Rooted in non-Western spatial perspectives, the work challenges how the body perceives space, time, and existence — rejecting fixed vantage points.

03

Body Immersion

The body itself becomes part of the art. Works respond to touch, movement, and presence — every visitor transforms the experience for all others.
